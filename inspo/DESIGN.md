## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a high-performance, dark-mode corporate aesthetic engineered for digital agencies, creative technology platforms, and modern creative studios. Inspired by deep volumetric polygon facets, luminous ethereal smoke effects, and electric blue luminescence, the visual language balances technical precision with high-impact visual energy.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Tech Minimalism** with **Luminescent Glassmorphism**. Dark midnight surfaces provide an anchor, while electric cobalt, vivid neon cyan, and crisp pure white elements establish immediate visual priority. The emotional response is one of confidence, cutting-edge innovation, and polished creative power.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an abyss of deep midnight navies and obsidian blues, punctuated by charged electric blues and bright cyan highlights:

- **Primary (`#0066FF`)**: The foundational active hue. Used for core interactive states, focus rings, primary actions, and saturated graphic fills.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF`)**: An intense electric cyan derived from glowing smoke filaments. Used for key accents, radiant gradients, badge highlights, and high-priority indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0088FF`)**: Cobalt blue bridge tone that blends transitions between primary blue and bright cyan in interactive gradients.
- **Neutral Base (`#0A0F1D`)**: The primary deep dark surface backdrop, reinforced by secondary midnight tier `#0D1527` and surface containers ranging from `#131C35` to `#1B2748`.
- **Text & Accents**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for bold titles and high-contrast labels, supported by slate-tinted cool grays (`#94A3B8` and `#64748B`) for secondary prose.

## Typography
The typographic identity pairs **Outfit** for structural, punchy, geometric headlines with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for crisp, highly-legible body text and UI microcopy.

- **Headlines (Outfit)**: Pure geometric construction with bold, wide apertures that echo modern technical identity and dynamic visual branding.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Designed with balanced x-heights and open counters to preserve absolute legibility against dark, high-contrast backgrounds without causing eye strain.
- **Uppercasing**: Reserved strictly for `label-sm` and kicker subtitles with extended letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror premium corporate signage.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a flexible 12-column grid system built on an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a supplementary 4pt sub-grid for fine component alignment.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width container capped at 1280px or 1440px with 64px outside margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margin boundaries.

Card clusters and grouped metrics maintain strict internal padding hierarchies: 24px for desktop modules, collapsing down to 16px on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids murky black drop shadows, relying instead on **chromatic ambient glow**, **faceted dark surfaces**, and **luminous translucent glass borders**:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas)**: Solid background tone `#0A0F1D`.
- **Layer 1 (Sub-surface/Cards)**: Background `#0D1527` with a 1px structural stroke of `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Panels & Popovers)**: Translucent surface `#131C35` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Layer 3 (Active Focus & Modal Depth)**: High-altitude overlays supported by an ambient cyan-cobalt drop glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.35), 0 0 12px 0 rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes
The shape language implements a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** standard. Containers and surface panels feature an 8px (`0.5rem`) base radius, scaling to 16px (`1rem`) on primary content cards and dialogs.

Interactive touch points such as badges, pills, and standalone icon triggers utilize full-round capsules (`9999px`) to create an organic balance against the geometric polygon structure of the brand canvas.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid gradient fill from `#0066FF` to `#0088FF` with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), semi-bold weight, and a subtle electric cyan bottom inner glow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`). Hover generates an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary / Glass**: Background `rgba(13, 21, 39, 0.7)` with a crisp 1px border of `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.4)`, text in `#00D2FF`. Hover transitions background to `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)` and border to `#00D2FF`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, text in `#FFFFFF`, hover background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed using `#0D1527` or translucent glass `rgba(13, 21, 39, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Perimeter framed by a precise 1px stroke of `rgba(0, 136, 255, 0.2)`.
- On hover, borders brighten to `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.5)` with a faint interior gradient flash.

### Input Fields
- Base surface `#080C17` recessed with a 1px border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.
- Text color `#FFFFFF` with placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- Focused state features a crisp `#0088FF` border alongside an outer focus ring glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.25)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with a dark translucent tint `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.12)`.
- Border `1px solid rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`.
- Text styled in `label-sm` with vibrant electric cyan `#00D2FF`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Unchecked: `#0D1527` interior with `1.5px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.35)`.
- Checked: `#0066FF` fill with a pure white glyph and a bright cyan ambient drop-shadow (`0 0 8px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.6)`).
